# NeuroNetworks voice

Tagline: Learn, Develop, Create. Audience: parents and Class 10 pass students in India.

- Plain, specific, calm. Short sentences. Say hours, weeks, projects and exams, not "world-class".
- Lead with what the student does and builds each month.
- Never claim or imply guaranteed placement, internship, salary or exam pass. Certifications are optional, billed by the provider, and prices are reconfirmed before each window.
- No invented statistics, testimonials, mentor names or logos. Use bracketed placeholders such as [YOUR FEES] until the owner supplies facts.
- Name the brand "NeuroNetworks AI Ed" in text; the domain spelling is neuronetwrks.com.
- Address parents respectfully; for under-18s the parent or guardian is the contact.
- Avoid hype words, emoji and generic AI-startup phrasing.

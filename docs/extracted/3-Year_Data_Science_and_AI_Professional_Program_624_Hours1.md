**3-YEAR DATA SCIENCE & AI PROFESSIONAL PROGRAM**

**Absolute Beginner → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Professional / Job-ready**

**Class 10 Entry \| 3 Years \| 52 Weeks/Year \| 4 Hours/Week \| 624 Total Hours**

# 1. Program Purpose

A three-year, 624-hour Data Science & AI specialization for a Class-10-pass absolute beginner. The course progresses from computing, Python, SQL and statistics into analytics, machine learning, data engineering, deep learning, generative AI, MLOps and production-style projects. The target is job readiness through demonstrated skills, extensive hands-on work, GitHub and a professional portfolio. Internship, employment, salary and external certification are not guaranteed.

# 3. Exact Hour Model

Each year has 52 weeks. Eight months use 4 weeks and four months use 5 weeks, giving exactly 208 hours/year and 624 hours over three years. Every month ends with a one-hour examination.

| **Year** | **Weeks** | **Hours** | **5-week months**                |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Year 1   | 52        | 208       | March, June, September, December |
| Year 2   | 52        | 208       | March, June, September, December |
| Year 3   | 52        | 208       | March, June, September, December |
| Total    | 156       | 624       | 12 five-week months              |

# 4. Teaching & Assessment Model

- All concepts start from zero.

- Theory is followed by demonstration and independent hands-on work.

- Each month produces a portfolio artifact.

- Every major monthly topic ends in a one-hour theory/practical examination.

- Certification preparation is embedded into the technical modules.

- AI tools may assist work, but students must verify analysis, code and model outputs.

# 15. Program Notes

- All 624 hours include learning, practical work, projects, assignments, and the 36 one-hour monthly exams.

- The program starts from absolute zero and does not assume prior knowledge of programming, statistics, data science, or AI/ML. ( Basic 10<sup>th</sup> Grade math knowledge is expected).

- The curriculum progresses from Python, SQL, statistics and data analysis to machine learning, data engineering, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, RAG and MLOps.

- The program includes hands-on projects, portfolio work, real-world case studies, and capstone projects to demonstrate practical skills.

- Cloud platforms such as Azure ML may involve additional usage costs, depending on usage, region, and current pricing.

- External certifications, where applicable, are treated as preparation milestones; completing the curriculum does not automatically award external credentials.

- Certification fees, cloud costs, and third-party pricing are approximate and subject to change.

- Larger AI/ML workloads may require cloud GPU resources, particularly for deep learning, LLMs and advanced model training.

- No internship or job-placement guarantee is included.

- The final stages focus on production-oriented AI/ML, scalable data platforms, responsible AI, MLOps, real-world implementation, and a professional portfolio.

**3-YEAR SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT PROFESSIONAL PROGRAM**

**Absolute Beginner → Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Professional / Job-ready**

**Class 10 Entry \| 3 Years \| 52 Weeks/Year \| 4 Hours/Week \| 624 Total Hours**

# 1. Program Purpose

This three-year, 624-hour Software Development specialization is designed for a Class-10-pass student with no assumed programming knowledge. It progresses from computer and programming fundamentals to professional full-stack, cloud and software-engineering capability. The target is job readiness through practical skills, a high volume of hands-on work, GitHub evidence and a professional portfolio. Employment or internship placement is not guaranteed.

# 3. Exact Hour Model

The program uses 52 weeks per year. To preserve exactly 624 hours, each year contains eight 4-week months and four 5-week months. Each month has a one-hour internal examination; the remaining hours are learning and practical work.

| **Year** | **Weeks** | **Hours** | **5-week months**                |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Year 1   | 52        | 208       | March, June, September, December |
| Year 2   | 52        | 208       | March, June, September, December |
| Year 3   | 52        | 208       | March, June, September, December |
| Total    | 156       | 624       | 12 five-week months              |

# 4. Teaching & Assessment Model

- Theory is taught from zero; no prior programming or computer-science knowledge is assumed.

- Demonstration is followed by independent hands-on implementation.

- Every month produces a practical artifact suitable for GitHub/portfolio documentation.

- Each major monthly topic ends with a one-hour examination.

- Examinations may combine theory, coding, debugging, scenario questions and practical tasks.

- Certification objectives are embedded into the relevant technical modules rather than taught as isolated exam-cram blocks.

- AI tools may be used as development assistants, but students must understand, test and review the generated output.

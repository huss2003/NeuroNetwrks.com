## 2. Outreach email: schools and coaching centres

**Who to send it to:** principals, career counsellors and coordinators at CBSE, ICSE and state-board schools; owners of coaching centres for Classes 9 and 10. Start with schools you or people you know have a connection with.

**How to use it:**
1. Find the right person's name and email from the school website or by calling the office. Do not send to a generic "info@" address if you can avoid it.
2. Change everything in brackets. Send one email at a time, never a bulk email with everyone in "To".
3. If there is no reply after 7 days, send one short follow-up (template below). Then stop.
4. Track each school in a sheet: name, contact, date sent, follow-up date, reply.

Note: this is an offer of a free information session for students and parents, not a request for student contact details. Never ask a school to share student phone numbers or emails.

## 3. Backlink ideas (ethical only)

A backlink is a link to neuronetwrks.com from another website. Google treats links from real, relevant sites as votes of trust. The ideas below earn links by being useful.

**Never do these:** buy links, pay for "guest posts" on link-selling sites, swap links in bulk ("you link me, I link you"), use link networks or "DA 50+ backlink packages", post links in blog comments or forums as spam, or write fake reviews. Google can penalise the whole site for this, and it is very hard to recover. If someone offers to sell you backlinks, say no.

### What makes these work

- **Lead with something useful**: a free session, a free guide, a free template. Not "please link to us".
- **One clear link** to the most relevant page, not always the home page.
- **Keep a record** of every pitch in a sheet: site, contact, date, result, link URL.
- **Check new links** monthly in Search Console: **Links > Top linking sites**.

### What to say about the brand in any article or session

Use only facts from the curriculum: years, hours, modules, projects, tools, and the certifications the programs prepare for. Do not mention student numbers, results, placements, salaries, rankings or partnerships that are not confirmed in writing. The tagline is always **Learn · Develop · Create**.

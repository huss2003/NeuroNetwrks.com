# Free listings and profiles

NeuroNetworks AI Ed · neuronetwrks.com · Learn · Develop · Create

Free listings help in three ways: people find you on sites they already use, Google and AI search tools see the same facts in many places (which builds trust in a new brand), and most listings give a link back to the website.

**All listings here are free.** Several of these sites will offer paid "premium" or "featured" plans by phone or email after you sign up. You do not need them. Say no politely.

## Before you start: the NAP block

NAP means **Name, Address, Phone**. It must be written exactly the same on every listing, down to the spelling and punctuation. Copy it from here every time.

```
Name:     NeuroNetworks AI Ed
Address:  [address to be confirmed]
Phone:    [phone to be confirmed]
Email:    Hussain@neuronetwrks.com
Website:  https://neuronetwrks.com
```

- Always write **NeuroNetworks AI Ed** (not "Neuro Networks", "NeuroNetworks AI", or "Neuronetworks Academy").
- The website is spelled **neuronetwrks.com** (no "o" in "netwrks").
- Never type the placeholder text into a live listing. If a site requires an address or phone and you do not have one confirmed yet, wait.
- When the address and phone are confirmed, update this block first, then every listing, then tell Claude so the website matches.

## 1. Google Business Profile

**Only set this up once you have a confirmed address or service area and a phone number.** Google may suspend profiles with wrong or temporary details, and it is hard to undo.

**Start at:** https://www.google.com/business/

**Category:** Primary: **Computer training school**. Additional: **Educational institution**. (Type the word and pick from Google's list; you cannot invent categories.)

**Steps:**
1. Sign in with the Google account linked to Hussain@neuronetwrks.com and click **Manage now**.
2. Business name: **NeuroNetworks AI Ed** (exactly; do not add words like "Best AI Course" to the name, Google suspends that).
3. Category: Computer training school.
4. "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"
   - If students come to a centre: **Yes**, and enter the full address.
   - If they do not: **No**. Then choose **service areas** (cities or states you serve). Never list a home address.
5. Phone: [phone to be confirmed]. Website: https://neuronetwrks.com.
6. Verify. Google may ask for a video of the premises or a phone or postcard code. Follow its instructions.
7. After verification, open the profile and add:
   - **Description** (750 characters max): use the medium description above.
   - **Opening date:** [launch date].
   - **Hours:** the hours you answer enquiries.
   - **Services:** add the six course names from the table.
   - **Photos:** logo, the cover image, and real photos of a classroom or lab only once they exist. No stock photos.
8. Copy the profile link (Share > Copy link) and send it to Claude for the website's schema.

## A note on Wikidata and Wikipedia

**Do not create a Wikipedia article or Wikidata item yet.**

- Wikipedia only accepts articles about organisations that have **significant coverage in independent, reliable sources** (newspapers, education magazines, established news sites). The brand's own website, social profiles and directory listings do not count. An article created too early is usually deleted, and the deletion stays on record.
- Wikidata has a lower bar but still expects the item to be notable or backed by serious, public references. A brand-new item with only self-published sources may be deleted.
- Writing about your own organisation on Wikipedia is a conflict of interest under its rules.

**When to revisit:** once there are at least two or three independent articles about NeuroNetworks AI Ed in reliable publications (for example, after the press release in `pr-and-partnerships.md` is picked up by a newspaper). At that point, ask Claude to check the sources and help create a Wikidata item with those references. Leave Wikipedia to an independent editor.

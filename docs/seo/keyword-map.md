**Notes.**
- Avoid the head term "coding course" (14,800). Most of that demand is for medical coding.
- Write "cyber security" as two words in titles and copy, because that is how people search.
- "Diploma" is the most-searched modifier. Use it in titles only if the programs actually award a diploma, and confirm this with the owner first.
- "Salary" and "eligibility" questions appear in every cluster. Answer eligibility in the copy. Never state salary figures.

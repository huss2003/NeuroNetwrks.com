Six stages,
one rhythm.

Learn, practise, build, test and improve. This is the cycle behind both the one-year and the three-year programs.

  1. 01

    Learn

    A clear concept, then a worked example

  2. 02

    Guided practice

    Follow along with support

  3. 03

    Independent lab

    Solve real tasks yourself

  4. 04

    Practical exam

    A one-hour exam after each module

  5. 05

    Get feedback

    Review your results and hints

  6. 06

    Improve and retry

    Learn from mistakes and try again

AI Foundation · every lesson

Five steps
in every lesson.

AI Foundation runs 2 guided hours a week for 104 hours, plus 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture. Each lesson follows the same five steps.

  1. 01

    Concept

    One idea, explained simply.

  2. 02

    Worked example

    See it done before you do it.

  3. 03

    Scenario question

    Choose the best answer in a real situation.

  4. 04

    Guided practice

    Try it with support close by.

  5. 05

    Exit check

    Confirm you are ready to move on.

Three-year programs · every month

Five beats
in every month.

The three-year programs run 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year. Exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

  1. 01

    Theory

    Short explanations of the month's topic.

  2. 02

    Demonstration

    An instructor works through an example on screen.

  3. 03

    Hands-on lab

    You repeat and extend it yourself.

  4. 04

    Project

    A piece of work you finish and can show.

  5. 05

    Exam

    A one-hour exam closes the month.

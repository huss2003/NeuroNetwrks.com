From theory to real skills

Five steps
in every lesson.

Every lesson follows the same flow, so you always know what comes next. You learn a concept, see it worked through, practise it, answer a question on your own and finish with a quick check.

  1. Step 01

    Concept

    One idea, explained simply.

  2. Step 02

    Worked example

    See it done before you do it.

  3. Step 03

    Scenario question

    Choose the best answer in a real situation.

  4. Step 04

    Guided practice

    Try it with support close by.

  5. Step 05

    Exit check

    Confirm you are ready to move on.

AI Foundation: 2 guided hours a week for 104 hours, plus 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.

A structured three-year journey

Five beats
in every month.

Each month ends with a project and a one-hour exam. The three-year programs run 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

  1. 01

    Theory

    Short explanations of the month's topic.

  2. 02

    Demonstration

    An instructor works through an example on screen.

  3. 03

    Hands-on lab

    You repeat and extend it yourself.

  4. 04

    Project

    A piece of work you finish and can show.

  5. 05

    Exam

    A one-hour exam closes the month.

AI, software or data science?A guide for parents

Three tech paths sound similar but lead to different daily work. This guide is written for parents deciding with their child after Class 10.

In short

  • AI/ML engineering builds AI systems; software development builds applications; data science answers questions with data.
  • All three are 3 years, 624 hours, 4 hours a week, with a project and a one-hour exam every month.
  • Not sure yet? The 6-month AI Foundation course lets a student try Python, data and AI first.

What is the difference in one line each?

AI/ML Engineering
Builds systems that learn from data: models, deep learning, generative AI, agents and MLOps.
Software Development
Builds websites and applications: Java, JavaScript, React, databases, APIs and cloud.
Data Science & AI
Answers questions with data: SQL, statistics, dashboards, machine learning and data pipelines.

In the 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey, Python was the most-used language among people learning to code (72%). It is the main language in both AI/ML Engineering and Data Science & AI.

How can a parent tell which suits their child?

  • If your child enjoys building and fixing things on a computer, software development fits.
  • If they ask how ChatGPT or face unlock works, AI/ML engineering fits.
  • If they like charts, sports statistics or spotting patterns, data science fits.
  • If you cannot tell yet, AI Foundation (6 months, 4 hours a week) is a sensible first step.

Each path has a step-by-step roadmap: how to become an AI engineer after 10th, how to become a software engineer after 10th and how to become a data scientist after 10th.

What should parents check before enrolling?

  • The total guided hours and weekly time commitment.
  • What is built and assessed each month.
  • Recommended laptop specification.
  • Fees, intake dates and batch timings (ask us).
  • How the institute handles consent and data for students under 18.

Frequently asked questions

Which has better career scope: AI, software or data science?
All three are active fields. Scope depends far more on the skills and portfolio a student builds than on the label. No program guarantees a job, internship, salary or exam pass. Certification exams are run and awarded by the certifying organisations.
Can my child switch paths later?
The first months of all three overlap in computer and programming foundations, so early skills carry over. Ask us about switching when you enquire.
Who do we contact for a student under 18?
For students under 18, a parent or guardian is the main contact. See admissions.

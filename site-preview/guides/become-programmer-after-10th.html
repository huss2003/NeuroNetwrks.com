In short

  • Programming is a thinking skill before it is a typing skill. Logic comes before any language.
  • Pick one first language and go deep. In this roadmap that is Java, right after programming logic.
  • Daily practice beats long weekend sessions. Solving small problems often is how coders improve.

A programmer, often called a coder, turns a problem into exact instructions a computer can follow. The core skill is breaking a big task into small steps, writing them in a language such as Java, testing them and fixing what breaks. Every software, web, app or AI role is built on top of this skill.

Can you start on the path to a programmer right after Class 10?

Yes. Class 10 is a good time to start programming, because you only need logic, patience and a laptop, and the habit of solving small problems every week compounds over the following years.

Programming fits every stream, but your stream decides which degree you can take later. Here is the honest comparison.

Which skills does a programmer need?

A programmer needs step-by-step logic, one language learned well, object-oriented thinking, data structures and algorithms, debugging and testing habits, Git, and the judgement to use AI coding tools without losing understanding of the code.

Nasscom's Strategic Review 2026 says AI has moved from optional to core infrastructure in India's tech industry. For new programmers, that makes understanding code more important, which is why this roadmap teaches logic first and AI-assisted coding last.

You build a project every month, and for a programmer the key ones are a 50-problem logic repository, Java command-line and OOP programs, two data structures repositories and a final interview challenge set.

Who should not choose this path?

Programming is not for you if you want quick results without practice, if being stuck on one problem for an hour feels unbearable, or if you are looking for a guaranteed job.

  • You want to learn by watching only. Coding is learned by typing, failing and fixing. Videos alone do not build the skill.
  • Being stuck feels unbearable. Every programmer spends time stuck. The skill is staying calm and trying the next idea.
  • You cannot practise regularly. Plan for 4 hours of classes a week for three years, plus your own practice.
  • You want a guarantee. This program does not promise a job, salary or exam pass.
  • You want a degree from this course. It awards a NeuroNetworks AI Ed certificate, not a degree. Many students take it alongside school and college.

What should parents ask before choosing a course?

Parents should ask five things of any course: can I see the full curriculum, what will my child build, how is learning checked, who answers questions, and what exactly is promised.

  1. Can I see the whole curriculum, month by month? A good course shows every module and its hours before you pay.
  2. What will my child build, and where will I see it? Ask for project names and whether work goes on GitHub.
  3. How is learning checked, and how often? Regular exams and practicals show progress early, not only at the end.
  4. Who answers questions, and how quickly? Ask how doubts are handled between classes.
  5. What is promised, and what is not? Get the certificate, timings and fees in writing. Be careful of anyone who guarantees a job or salary.

Frequently asked questions

How many years does it take to become a programmer after 10th?

You can write simple programs within weeks. Becoming job-ready usually takes years of practice. This roadmap uses three years and 624 hours, and many students also finish a degree.

Can I become a programmer without maths?

Yes. Basic programming needs logic, not advanced maths. Algorithms do use maths-style thinking, and B.Tech through JEE Main needs Physics and Mathematics in Class 12, so keeping maths helps.

Which programming language should I learn first?

Pick one and go deep. In this roadmap it is Java, after a month of pure programming logic, because it teaches clear structure and object-oriented thinking. JavaScript follows later in year 1.

Is a programmer the same as a coder?

In everyday use, yes. Both mean someone who writes and fixes code. Job titles such as developer or software engineer usually add design, testing and teamwork on top.

How much should I practise coding each week?

Steady practice matters more than long sessions. The program has 4 hours of classes a week, and short, regular problem solving between classes is what builds speed.

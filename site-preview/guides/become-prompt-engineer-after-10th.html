In short

  • Prompt engineering is a skill, rarely a whole job. It sits inside AI development, AI engineering and many other roles.
  • One year is enough for a strong start. AI Foundation covers prompting, evaluation, context engineering, RAG and agents in 104 hours.
  • To make it a career, add coding. Python and AI app building turn good prompts into products.

Prompt engineering is the skill of instructing AI models so they give accurate, safe and useful answers, and then testing those answers. In most companies it is one skill inside AI developer or AI engineer roles. Few jobs are only prompt writing, so it works best as a starting point.

Can you start on the path to a prompt engineer right after Class 10?

Yes, and you can start even earlier: prompting needs clear thinking and good English more than maths, and AI Foundation teaches it in one year from Class 8 with no coding needed.

Since prompting is a skill rather than a degree subject, any stream works. What changes is which career you can grow it into.

Which skills does a prompt engineer need?

A prompt engineer needs a clear understanding of how LLMs work, precise writing, prompting techniques, ways to evaluate AI answers, context and RAG design, basic Python for testing at scale, and a strong sense of AI safety and bias.

Nasscom's Strategic Review 2026 describes AI as moving from optional to core infrastructure in India's tech industry. As AI spreads into daily work, the ability to instruct and check it is useful in many roles.

What will you build on the way?

You build a project most months, and for prompt engineering the most useful ones test prompts fairly, ground answers in documents, drive an agent, and end in a deployed app on GitHub.

These are real projects from the AI Foundation roadmap, in the order a student meets them:

  1. Practical AI audit. Checks where AI tools help and where they fail, the base of good prompting.
  2. Responsible AI workshop. Builds the habit of spotting bias and unsafe output.
  3. Small generative AI application. Your prompts working inside a real app for the first time.
  4. Prompt evaluation benchmark project. Compares prompts with a fair test, the core skill of this role.
  5. RAG Knowledge Assistant project. Shows that good context beats clever wording.
  6. AI workflow and agent project. Uses prompts to drive a multi-step task with tools.
  7. Final capstone project and graduation. A finished AI project on GitHub that shows your prompting and testing.

A parent can open the GitHub page at any time and see the work. That is often more convincing than a marksheet.

Which certificates help a future prompt engineer?

AI Foundation prepares you for two optional exams, Microsoft AI-901 and AWS AIF-C01, which test wider AI knowledge rather than prompting alone, and each is awarded only by Microsoft or AWS after their own exam.

The AI Foundation program prepares students for these 2. Each is awarded by its own certifying body after its own exam, and passing is not guaranteed.

  • Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901). Aimed at people starting a career in AI solution development. It expects some Python syntax and gives 55–60% of its weight to building AI solutions with Microsoft Foundry.
  • AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01). A foundational exam on AI, ML and generative AI for people who use AI on AWS but do not necessarily build it: 65 questions in 90 minutes.

Google AI Essentials, part of the AI/ML Engineering roadmap, is a beginner course needing no experience. One of its five courses is on prompting, including few-shot prompting.

Who should not choose this path?

Prompt engineering is not for you if you want a large, standalone job title from prompts alone, dislike testing your own work, or hope to skip coding entirely for a long-term technical career.

  • You want a guarantee. No honest course promises a job, salary or exam pass. This one does not.
  • You expect a big job market for prompts alone. Most companies want prompting as part of a wider role, such as AI developer.
  • You do not like checking your own work. Good prompt engineers test, measure and rewrite again and again.
  • You want to avoid coding forever. For technical AI careers, Python is what turns prompts into products.
  • You only have a phone. The Python labs need a laptop or desktop with a browser.

Other paths to compare: AI/ML Engineering, Software Development, Data Science & AI, Product & UX / AI Design, Cybersecurity.

What should parents ask before choosing a course?

Parents should ask five things of any course: can I see the full curriculum, what will my child build, how is learning checked, who answers questions, and what exactly is promised.

  1. Can I see the whole curriculum, month by month? A good course shows every module and its hours before you pay.
  2. What will my child build, and where will I see it? Ask for project names and whether work goes on GitHub.
  3. How is learning checked, and how often? Regular exams and practicals show progress early, not only at the end.
  4. Who answers questions, and how quickly? Ask how doubts are handled between classes.
  5. What is promised, and what is not? Get the certificate, timings and fees in writing. Be careful of anyone who guarantees a job or salary.

Frequently asked questions

How many years does it take to become a prompt engineer after 10th?

The prompting skill itself can be built in one year, such as the 104-hour AI Foundation course. Turning it into a career usually means more: a three-year path like AI/ML Engineering, and often a degree.

Can I become a prompt engineer without maths?

Yes, for prompting itself. It needs clear writing, logic and testing more than maths. AI Foundation needs no coding or advanced maths. Technical AI roles that use prompting do need more maths later.

Is prompt engineering a real job in India?

It is a real and useful skill. It usually sits inside roles such as AI developer, AI engineer, or content and operations work that uses AI. Standalone prompt engineer titles are less common, so plan to combine it with other skills.

Can I learn prompt engineering in Class 8 or 9?

Yes. AI Foundation is open from Class 8, with no prior coding. It runs for one year at 2 hours a week, with 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.

Is AI Foundation enough to get a job as a prompt engineer?

No. AI Foundation is a project-based foundation in AI and Python, not a job-ready program. It does not guarantee a job, internship or exam pass. Students who want depth can continue to AI/ML Engineering after Class 10.

Becoming an AI engineer is a multi-year path. Here are the steps in order, with the hours and projects for each.

In short

  • The path is: Python, maths for machine learning, data preparation, classical ML, deep learning, generative AI and MLOps.
  • The AI/ML Engineering program covers these steps over 3 years and 624 hours, with a project every month.
  • A portfolio of 6 to 10 flagship projects is the main evidence of skill at the end.

What does an AI engineer do?

An AI engineer builds software that uses machine learning and generative AI: preparing data, training or adapting models, testing them and running them reliably for real users. The work combines programming, mathematics and engineering practice such as version control, containers, cloud services and monitoring.

Step 1: Learn Python and computer basics

Start with how computers work and Python programming. In AI/ML Engineering this is the first three months: Computer + AI Foundations, Python Fundamentals and Python for AI.

Step 2: Learn the maths and data skills for ML

Machine learning rests on statistics, linear algebra and careful data handling. The program covers Math for ML I and II, then Data Preparation, before any models are trained.

Step 3: Machine learning foundations

Next come ML foundations, model evaluation, classical ML and unsupervised ML, ending Year 1 with a capstone. Projects include a house-price regression model, a customer-risk classifier and customer segmentation.

Step 4: Deep learning, vision and language

Year 2 covers deep learning with PyTorch and TensorFlow, computer vision, NLP, transformers and MLOps. One project is an image classification application.

Step 5: Generative AI, agents and a portfolio

Year 3 covers generative AI, embeddings and RAG, agents, fine-tuning, AI safety, cloud AI engineering and system design, ending with a portfolio of 6 to 10 flagship projects and a final capstone. Examples are the document RAG assistant and AgentFlow AI.

Not ready for 3 years?

The 1-year AI Foundation course (104 hours, 2 hours a week) covers Python, data, machine learning, generative AI, RAG and AI agents, ending with a deployed AI application and a capstone, and is a good test of interest first.

What can you earn in India?

Entry-level roles in this field typically pay ₹5–10 LPA a year in India, rising to ₹12–25 LPA with 3–5 years' experience and ₹30–60+ LPA at senior level. These are indicative market ranges, not guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

How many years does it take to become an AI engineer after 10th?
The AI/ML Engineering program takes three years at four hours a week (624 hours). Many students also pursue a degree; skills and a project portfolio are built alongside.
Is maths compulsory for AI engineering?
Yes, to a working level. The program teaches the needed maths in two dedicated modules, starting from school-level mathematics.
What should I learn first for AI?
Python. It is the first programming language in both AI Foundation and AI/ML Engineering.

In short

  • This is software work with AI inside. Good coding habits matter as much as AI knowledge.
  • Plan for about three years after Class 10, or start earlier with the one-year AI Foundation course from Class 8.
  • Your portfolio is your proof. Working apps that use AI APIs, RAG and agents say more than marks.

An AI developer builds apps and tools that use AI models made by others. A typical task is connecting a language model through an API, giving it your own documents with RAG, or letting an agent call tools. The focus is the software around the model, not inventing new models.

Can you start on the path to an AI developer right after Class 10?

Yes. You can start right after Class 10, and if you are younger, the one-year AI Foundation course is open from Class 8 and already covers AI APIs, RAG and agents at a beginner level.

Because AI development is mostly coding, your stream choice affects degrees more than skills. Here is the honest picture for each.

For the NITs and other institutes that fill B.E. and B.Tech seats through JEE Main, the 2026 rules ask for Physics, Mathematics, a language, one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology or a technical vocational subject, and one more subject in Class 12. State colleges can set their own rules.

Students in Class 8 or 9 can take the one-year, 104-hour AI Foundation course, which ends with a deployed AI app. After Class 10, AI/ML Engineering goes much deeper.

What is the year-by-year roadmap to become an AI developer?

Which skills does an AI developer need?

An AI developer needs strong Python, Git, working with APIs, prompt and context design, embeddings and RAG, agent and tool design, cloud AI services on Azure and AWS, testing of AI output, and responsible AI habits.

Nasscom's Strategic Review 2026 says AI has moved from optional to core infrastructure across India's tech industry. More companies now need developers who can put AI inside everyday software.

What will you build on the way?

You build a project every month for 36 months, and for an AI developer the standouts are apps that call AI services, answer from documents, use tools as agents and run on the cloud.

These are real projects from the AI/ML Engineering roadmap, in the order a student meets them:

  1. CLI utility collection. Small Python tools that build the coding habits app work needs.
  2. Azure AI mini solution. Your first app built on a ready-made cloud AI service.
  3. AWS AI service architecture project. Plans how AWS AI services fit into one working app.
  4. Transformer text application. A text app built on a modern language model.
  5. Document RAG assistant. An assistant that answers from your own files, the most common AI app today.
  6. Tool-using AI agent. An agent that calls tools to finish a task, not just chat.
  7. Multimodal document assistant. Reads text and images together, like a real office document tool.
  8. AI safety test suite. Tests that check your app's answers before users rely on them.

A parent can open the GitHub page at any time and see the work. That is often more convincing than a marksheet.

Which certificates help an future AI developer?

Microsoft AI-901 suits AI developers best because over half of it is about building with Microsoft Foundry, and IBM AI Engineering adds RAG and LangChain, though each certificate is awarded only by its own body.

The AI/ML Engineering program prepares students for these 5. Each is awarded by its own certifying body after its own exam, and passing is not guaranteed.

  • Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901). Weighted 40–45% on AI concepts and 55–60% on implementing AI solutions with Microsoft Foundry. Microsoft says some Python syntax knowledge is needed.
  • AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01).
  • Google AI Essentials.
  • NVIDIA DLI Fundamentals of Deep Learning.
  • IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate. Includes a course on AI agents using RAG and LangChain, and a final project building generative AI applications with RAG and LangChain.

AI-901 preparation is also part of the final month of AI Foundation, so a younger student can prepare for it within one year.

What laptop do you need to become an AI developer?

You need a laptop or desktop with internet, because a phone alone is not enough for the labs. This is the setup recommended for the AI/ML Engineering program.

  • CPU: Intel i5 / Ryzen 5 or better
  • RAM: 16 GB minimum, 32 GB recommended
  • Storage: 512 GB SSD minimum, 1 TB recommended
  • GPU: Integrated GPU for basic ML; NVIDIA RTX 4060 8 GB+ recommended for the full roadmap
  • OS: Windows 11 + WSL2 or Linux

These are recommendations, not entry requirements. Ask us if your current computer is enough to start.

Who should not choose this path?

AI development is not for you if you dislike coding, want to research new models rather than use them, or expect a guaranteed job once you can call an AI API.

  • You want a guarantee. No honest program promises a job, salary or exam pass. This one does not.
  • You do not enjoy coding. Most of the work is ordinary software: APIs, data, errors and tests.
  • You want to invent new models. That is closer to ML research. The ML engineer guide fits better.
  • You think prompts alone are enough. Employers want working, tested apps, not only clever prompts.
  • You are not ready for regular practice. AI APIs change often. You need steady weekly time to keep up.

Other paths to compare: AI Foundation, Software Development, Data Science & AI, Product & UX / AI Design, Cybersecurity.

What should parents ask before choosing a course?

Parents should ask five things of any course: can I see the full curriculum, what will my child build, how is learning checked, who answers questions, and what exactly is promised.

  1. Can I see the whole curriculum, month by month? A good course shows every module and its hours before you pay.
  2. What will my child build, and where will I see it? Ask for project names and whether work goes on GitHub.
  3. How is learning checked, and how often? Regular exams and practicals show progress early, not only at the end.
  4. Who answers questions, and how quickly? Ask how doubts are handled between classes.
  5. What is promised, and what is not? Get the certificate, timings and fees in writing. Be careful of anyone who guarantees a job or salary.

What can an AI developer earn in India?

Indicative India ranges for AI/ML roles, including GenAI developer and AI solutions developer, are about ₹5 to 10 lakh a year for freshers, ₹12 to 25 lakh at 3 to 5 years, and ₹30 to 60 lakh or more for senior roles.

Frequently asked questions

How many years does it take to become an AI developer after 10th?

Plan for about three years of steady learning, such as the 3-year, 624-hour AI/ML Engineering program. Many employers also ask for a degree, so most students build these skills alongside Class 11, 12 and college.

Can I become an AI developer without maths?

You need less maths than an ML engineer, because you build on existing models. Logic and basic statistics still help. Keeping maths in Class 11 and 12 keeps the B.Tech route through JEE Main open.

What is the difference between an AI developer and an AI engineer?

An AI developer mainly builds apps on top of AI APIs and models. An AI engineer often also trains, evaluates and runs models and designs larger AI systems. Titles vary by company.

Can I start in Class 8 or 9?

Yes. AI Foundation is a one-year, 104-hour course at 2 hours a week, open from Class 8 with no coding needed. It covers Python, prompt and context engineering, RAG, AI APIs and agents, and a deployed AI app.

Which programming language should an AI developer learn first?

Python. Both AI Foundation and AI/ML Engineering use it from the start, because most AI APIs and libraries are built for it.

How to become a UI/UX designerafter 10th

UI/UX design is about making products easy and pleasant to use. Here is how a student can learn it from Class 10, including designing AI products.

In short

  • UI/UX design after Class 10 starts with visual design and user research, then wireframes, interaction design and prototyping.
  • The Product & UX / AI Design program is 3 years and 624 hours, with no design background assumed.
  • Years 2 and 3 add product management, design systems, AI UX patterns and a portfolio of 6 to 10 flagship projects.

What is UI/UX design?

UX (user experience) design is deciding how a product works for the people using it: what they need, how they move through it and where they get stuck. UI (user interface) design is how it looks: layout, type, colour and components. Together they turn research about real users into screens that are clear and easy to use.

What does the 3-year course cover?

The Product & UX / AI Design program, one module per month:

  1. Year 1 (208 hours): Design + Computer Foundations, Visual Design Fundamentals, UX Foundations + IxDF, User Research I, User Research II, Information Architecture, Wireframing, Interaction Design, UI Design I + CalArts, UI Design II, Prototyping, Year-1 UX Capstone + Google UX.
  2. Year 2 (208 hours): Product Fundamentals + Google PM, Product Discovery, Agile + Project Planning, Roadmaps + Metrics, Design Systems I, Design Systems II, AI Product Fundamentals, AI UX Patterns, AI Product Discovery, AI Prototyping, Microsoft AI Product Manager I, Microsoft AI Product Manager II.
  3. Year 3 (208 hours): AI Research + Evaluation, Advanced UX Research, AI Product Capstone, Advanced UX + NN/g Preparation, Accessibility + Inclusive AI, Complex Product Design, AI Design Systems, Product Analytics + Experimentation, AI Product Strategy, NN/g / Advanced UX Portfolio, GitHub + Portfolio Engineering, Final Professional Product + UX Capstone.

Where does AI come in?

From Year 2, students study AI product fundamentals, AI UX patterns, AI product discovery and AI prototyping, and in Year 3 AI research and evaluation, accessible and inclusive AI, and AI design systems. They learn to design products that use AI well, such as an AI assistant prototype, not just to use AI tools.

What do students build?

Examples include recreate 5 familiar app screens, information architecture project, clickable product prototype, product roadmap + kpi plan, ai product requirements document, advanced research case study, ai design system and flagship ai product design capstone.

Which certifications does it prepare for?

IxDF UX Certification, Google UX Design Professional Certificate, CalArts UI/UX Design Specialization, Google Project Management Professional Certificate, Microsoft AI Product Manager Professional Certificate and NN/g UX Certification. No program guarantees a job, internship, salary or exam pass. Certification exams are run and awarded by the certifying organisations.

What can you earn in India?

Entry-level roles in this field typically pay ₹4–8 LPA a year in India, rising to ₹10–22 LPA with 3–5 years' experience and ₹25–50+ LPA at senior level. These are indicative market ranges, not guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to draw well for UI/UX design?
No. UI/UX is about solving problems for users. Visual design fundamentals are taught in Year 1, and the program assumes no design background.
Is coding needed for UI/UX design?
Coding is not the focus of this program. Students learn research, wireframing, prototyping and design systems. Those who want to build interfaces in code can look at Software Development.
How long is the UI/UX course?
Three years, 624 hours, four hours a week, with a project and a one-hour exam every month.

Who is eligible to join?

All five three-year programs are designed for students who have passed Class 10. The one-year AI Foundation course also takes Class 10 pass (or equivalent) learners with no prior coding, AI or cloud experience.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply form on this site. [What happens after submission and the response time: to be confirmed.]

Do the programs guarantee a job or internship?

No. The programs aim at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but no internship, employment or salary is guaranteed.

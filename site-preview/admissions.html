Before you apply

Students first,
parents informed.

What to check before you send the form, whether you are the student or a parent or guardian.

For students

Before you apply.

  • You are in Class 9 or above for AI Foundation, or Class 11 or above for a three-year program.
  • You have a laptop or desktop with internet. See each program page for its recommended setup.
  • You know which program you are interested in, or you are happy to ask us.
Compare programs
For parents and guardians

Applying for a student under 18.

  • A parent or guardian applies and gives consent.
  • We collect only what is needed to respond, use unticked consent boxes, and do not track or advertise to children. See our privacy policy.
  • At admission we ask for the student's latest marksheet, a photo ID for the student and for the parent or guardian, and a signed consent form. Nothing else.
  • Enquiry details are deleted 12 months after our last contact if you do not join. A parent can ask us to delete them sooner by writing to Enquire@neuronetwrks.com.

Admissions FAQ.

Short answers to what most families ask first.

All admissions questions
Who is eligible to join?

All five three-year programs are designed for students from Class 11 onwards. The six-month AI Foundation course is open from Class 9, with no prior coding, AI or cloud experience needed.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply form on this site. We reply by email with the next steps.

Do the programs guarantee a job or internship?

No. The programs aim at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but no internship, employment or salary is guaranteed.

How we teach

Four principles.

  1. 01

    Practical first

    Every program is built around hands-on labs and projects, not lectures alone. Learners finish something real in every month.

  2. 02

    Measured, not guessed

    Every month closes with a one-hour exam, and AI Foundation uses an 80% pass mark with review and a retry. Progress is checked, and mistakes lead to review and another try.

  3. 03

    Honest about outcomes

    No program guarantees a job, internship or salary. We teach skills and help you build a portfolio and a GitHub record.

  4. 04

    Open to beginners

    Programs start from Class 10 and assume no prior coding, AI or design experience. Each program begins at the beginning.

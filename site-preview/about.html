Who we are

Our story.

We aim to teach critical, up-to-date Super Intelligence skills to students from Class 9 onwards, undergraduate students and working professionals. Our learners improve their skills, prepare for industry-recognised certifications and improve their chances of employment across industries, including but not limited to IT and ITES.

The gaps in the education system deprive learners of these critical skills. This skill gap differentiates a professional who has industry-relevant skills, and is therefore employable, from a professional who has not acquired them. We at Neuronetworks aspire to skill those who are willing, so that they are either employment-ready or able to pursue entrepreneurship.

Our five streams: Software Development, AI/ML Engineering, Data Science & AI, Cybersecurity and Product and UX/UI Design, with the AI Foundation course as the first step.

Mission, vision and values

What we stand for.

The purpose behind every program we teach.

Our mission

To skill as many willing people as possible in one or more of the five streams of Super Intelligence, so their skills meet the needs of any industry that uses it, and so they can gain employment or start their own venture.

Our vision

To be the institution of choice for learners who want to upgrade their skills in Super Intelligence and add value to their lives, their stakeholders and India.

A place where all seekers of Super Intelligence choose to learn, all employees wish to work, and all stakeholders wish to own.

About our guides

How our guides
are written.

Our guides are written by the Neuronetworks AI Ed team for students and parents.

  • Program facts come from our curriculum. Hours, modules, projects and certificates are taken from the published program roadmaps, never estimated.
  • Outside facts are sourced. Every guide lists the official pages we checked, such as certification bodies, the National Testing Agency and Nasscom, with the date we checked them.
  • We review before publishing. Each guide is checked against the program data and our rules: no invented numbers, no fees, and no promise of jobs, salaries or exam passes.
  • We update them. Each guide shows the date it was last updated. If a fact changes, we correct the page.

Information on this site

We work to keep program details, hours, modules and projects accurate and current. They come from our own curriculum and can change. Fees, intake dates and batch timings are shared on enquiry and confirmed in writing when you apply.

No guarantee of jobs, salaries or exam passes

Our programs teach skills and help students build a portfolio. No program guarantees a job, internship, salary or exam pass. Salary figures on this site are indicative market ranges, not promises. Pay depends on the employer, city, role and skills.

Certificates

Students who complete a program receive a Neuronetworks AI Ed certificate. External certifications, such as those from Microsoft, AWS, Google, CompTIA or ISC2, are awarded by those organisations after their own exams. Our programs prepare students for them, and passing is not guaranteed.

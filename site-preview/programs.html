Where should a beginner start?

AI Foundation is a beginner course of 104 hours over one year, from AI literacy to Python, data, machine learning and small AI applications.

How long are the specialisations?

Each is three years and 624 hours: 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Exam time is counted inside those hours.

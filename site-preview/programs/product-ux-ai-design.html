Overview

What this program is.

The Product & UX / AI Design program is a three-year, 624-hour specialisation for students who have passed Class 10. It begins with visual and UX fundamentals, moves through product thinking and project management, and ends with AI-assisted product design, research, design systems, prototyping and a professional portfolio.

Intended learner

Class 10 pass; no design background assumed.

How it is assessed

One 1-hour exam after each of the 36 monthly modules (theory plus practical where appropriate). Exam time is included in the 624 hours.

Questions

About this program.

Who can join this program?

Class 10 pass; no design background assumed. The program is designed for students entering after Class 10.

How many hours is the program, and how is it structured?

It is 624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Each monthly module ends with a one-hour exam, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

Does the program guarantee a job or internship?

No. The curriculum aims at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but it does not guarantee an internship, employment or salary.

Which external certifications does the roadmap include?

The roadmap lists six external certifications: IxDF UX Certification; Google UX Design Professional Certificate; CalArts UI/UX Design Specialization; Google Project Management Professional Certificate; Microsoft AI Product Manager Professional Certificate; NN/g UX Certification. They are awarded by the certifying organisations, not by this program, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.

What laptop do students need?

The curriculum document gives minimum specifications: CPU: Intel Core i5 / Ryzen 5 or better; RAM: 16 GB minimum; Storage: 512 GB SSD; GPU: Integrated graphics is sufficient — no dedicated GPU needed; Display: 14–16" Full HD, preferably IPS.

What kinds of roles does this program prepare students for?

The curriculum lists entry-level roles such as Junior UX Designer, Junior UI Designer, UX Research Assistant / Junior UX Researcher, Product Designer — Junior. Fit depends on the student's portfolio and on each employer's requirements.

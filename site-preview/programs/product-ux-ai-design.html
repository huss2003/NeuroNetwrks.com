Overview

What this program is.

The Product and UX/UI Design program is a three-year, 624-hour specialisation for students from Class 11 onwards. It begins with visual and UX fundamentals, moves through product thinking and project management, and ends with AI-assisted product design, research, design systems, prototyping and a professional portfolio.

Intended learner

Class 11 onwards; no design background assumed.

How it is assessed

One 1-hour exam after each of the 36 monthly modules (theory plus practical where appropriate). Exam time is included in the 624 hours.

Earning potential

What can you earn in India?

Entry-level roles linked to Product and UX/UI Design typically pay ₹4–8 LPA in India, rising with experience to ₹25–50+ LPA at senior level. These are market estimates, not guarantees.

Freelance work is another route: indicative rates are ₹500–1,500 an hour early on, rising to ₹3,000–6,000+ for advanced work.

Questions

About this program.

Who can join this program?

Class 11 onwards; no design background assumed. The program is open to students from Class 11 onwards.

How many hours is the program, and how is it structured?

It is 624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Each monthly module ends with a one-hour exam, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

Does the program guarantee a job or internship?

No. The curriculum aims at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but it does not guarantee an internship, employment or salary.

Which external certifications does the roadmap include?

The roadmap lists six external certifications: IxDF UX Certification; Google UX Design Professional Certificate; CalArts UI/UX Design Specialization; Google Project Management Professional Certificate; Microsoft AI Product Manager Professional Certificate; NN/g UX Certification. They are awarded by the certifying organisations, not by this program, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.

What laptop do students need?

Recommended laptop specifications (recommendations, not entry requirements): CPU: Intel Core i5 / Ryzen 5 or better; RAM: 16 GB minimum; Storage: 512 GB SSD; GPU: Integrated graphics is sufficient; no dedicated GPU needed; Display: 14–16" Full HD, preferably IPS.

What kinds of roles does this program prepare students for?

The curriculum lists entry-level roles such as Junior UX Designer, Junior UI Designer, UX Research Assistant / Junior UX Researcher, Junior Product Designer. Fit depends on the student's portfolio and on each employer's requirements.

What this program is.

The Cybersecurity program is a three-year, 624-hour specialisation for students who have passed Class 10, with no technical background assumed. It moves from computers and networking to Linux, security operations and ethical hacking, practised in authorised lab environments, with a one-hour exam after each of 36 monthly modules.

Intended learner

Class 10 pass; no technical background assumed.

How it is assessed

One 1-hour exam after each of the 36 monthly modules (theory plus practical where appropriate). Exam time is included in the 624 hours.

36 modules, twelve a year.

Each module is one month of the program. The curriculum page shows every module with its project.

About this program.

Who can join this program?

Class 10 pass; no technical background assumed. The program is designed for students entering after Class 10.

How many hours is the program, and how is it structured?

It is 624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Each monthly module ends with a one-hour exam, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

Does the program guarantee a job or internship?

No. The curriculum aims at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but it does not guarantee an internship, employment or salary.

Which external certifications does the roadmap include?

The roadmap lists six external certifications: ISC² Certified in Cybersecurity (CC); Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate; CompTIA Security+; CEH v13; CompTIA CySA+; Microsoft Security Operations Analyst Associate (SC-200). They are awarded by the certifying organisations, not by this program, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.

What laptop do students need?

The curriculum document gives minimum specifications: CPU: Intel Core i5 / Ryzen 5 or better; RAM: 16 GB minimum; 32 GB recommended; Storage: 512 GB SSD minimum; 1 TB recommended; GPU: Integrated graphics is sufficient — no dedicated GPU required; Network: Wi-Fi 6 + Gigabit Ethernet preferred.

What kinds of roles does this program prepare students for?

The curriculum lists entry-level roles such as SOC Analyst / SOC Analyst L1, Cybersecurity Analyst / Security Analyst, Information Security Analyst, Cyber Defense Analyst. Fit depends on the student's portfolio and on each employer's requirements.

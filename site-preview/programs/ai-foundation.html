QUESTIONS

About this program.

Who is the AI Foundations course for?

It is for learners who have passed Class 10 or equivalent, with no prior coding, AI or cloud experience. Learners should be able to read simple English instructions, use a browser and manage files.

How long is the course?

It is 104 guided hours over 52 weeks, at 2 hours a week. Months 1 to 8 are core learning (64 hours), Months 9 and 10 prepare for Microsoft AI-901 (20 hours), and Months 11 and 12 prepare for AWS AIF-C01 (20 hours).

Will I be certified, and does the course guarantee a pass?

The course prepares learners for two external exams, Microsoft AI-901 and AWS AIF-C01, but does not guarantee passing. Certification is awarded by Microsoft or AWS only after the learner passes their exam.

Does this make me an AI/ML engineer?

No. It is a beginner applied-AI foundation, not a full software engineering course, and it does not qualify a learner as an AI/ML engineer.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop or desktop with internet and a browser, able to install Python or use a browser-based notebook. A phone alone is not enough for the programming labs. Plan 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.

Overview

What this program is.

AI Foundation is a six-month, 104-hour course (26 weeks at 4 hours a week) open to students from Class 9, with no prior coding needed. It moves from AI basics and responsible use to real Python, data handling with Pandas, NumPy and Matplotlib, machine learning, computer vision and NLP, generative AI and LLMs, prompt and context engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), Python AI APIs and agents, and building, testing and deploying a complete AI application. The final month covers Microsoft AI-901 and AWS AIF-C01 exam preparation and a capstone project published to GitHub, without promising an exam pass.

Intended learner

Class 9 and above; no prior coding, AI or cloud experience required.

How it is assessed

104 one-hour lectures, each with 10 to 15 minutes of homework. A 30-question unit exam closes every major unit, with mandatory practicals in Module 4 (Python and Pandas data cleaning) and Module 5 (an end-to-end ML practical). Both certification tracks end in a full-length mock exam, and the course closes with a capstone presentation and practical assessment.

Questions

About this program.

Who is the AI Foundation course for?

It is open to students from Class 9 upwards, with no prior coding, AI or cloud experience. Students should be able to read simple English instructions, use a browser and manage files.

How long is the course?

It is 104 one-hour lectures over 26 weeks (six months), at 4 hours a week. Modules 1 to 11 are core learning (86 hours). The final module has 18 hours: 6 for Microsoft AI-901 preparation, 6 for AWS AIF-C01 preparation and 6 for the final capstone and graduation.

Will I be certified, and does the course guarantee a pass?

Students receive a Neuronetworks AI Ed certificate on completing the course. It also prepares students for two optional external exams, Microsoft AI-901 and AWS AIF-C01, but does not guarantee passing; those certifications are awarded by Microsoft or AWS after their exams.

What will I build?

A project in almost every module: a data-cleaning mini-project, an ML mini-project, a small generative AI app, a prompt evaluation benchmark, a RAG knowledge assistant, an AI workflow or agent, a complete tested and deployed AI application, and a final capstone published to GitHub.

Does this make me an AI/ML engineer?

No. It is a project-based foundation in AI and Python. Students who want depth can continue to a three-year specialisation such as AI/ML Engineering.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop or desktop with internet and a browser, able to install Python or use a browser-based notebook. A phone alone is not enough for the programming labs. Plan 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.

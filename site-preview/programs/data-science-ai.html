Overview

What this program is.

The Data Science & AI program is a three-year, 624-hour specialisation for Class 10 pass students who are absolute beginners. It progresses from computing, Python, SQL and statistics into analytics, machine learning, data engineering, deep learning, generative AI and MLOps, with a one-hour exam after every monthly module.

Intended learner

Class 10 pass; absolute beginners welcome.

How it is assessed

One 1-hour exam after each of the 36 monthly modules (theory plus practical where appropriate). Exam time is included in the 624 hours.

Questions

About this program.

Who can join this program?

Class 10 pass; absolute beginners welcome. The program is designed for students entering after Class 10.

How many hours is the program, and how is it structured?

It is 624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Each monthly module ends with a one-hour exam, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

Does the program guarantee a job or internship?

No. The curriculum aims at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but it does not guarantee an internship, employment or salary.

Which external certifications does the roadmap include?

The roadmap lists six external certifications: Microsoft Certified: Azure Data Fundamentals (DP-900); HackerRank SQL / Python Certificate; Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate; IBM Data Science Professional Certificate; Microsoft Certified: Azure Data Scientist Associate (DP-100); IBM Data Engineering Professional Certificate. They are awarded by the certifying organisations, not by this program, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.

What laptop do students need?

The curriculum document gives minimum specifications: CPU: Intel Core i7 / Ryzen 7 or equivalent; RAM: 32 GB; Storage: 1 TB NVMe SSD; GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4060/5060, 8 GB VRAM+; Display: 14–16" Full HD/2K IPS.

What kinds of roles does this program prepare students for?

The curriculum lists entry-level roles such as Junior Data Analyst, Data Analyst, BI Analyst / Junior BI Developer, Junior Data Scientist. Fit depends on the student's portfolio and on each employer's requirements.

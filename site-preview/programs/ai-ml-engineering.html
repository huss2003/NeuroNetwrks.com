Overview

What this program is.

AI/ML Engineering is a three-year, 624-hour specialisation for students who have passed Class 10. It starts with computer and Python basics and builds up to machine learning, deep learning, generative AI and MLOps, with a one-hour exam after each of 36 monthly modules and a project in every month.

Intended learner

Class 10 pass; basic school-level mathematics and computer familiarity.

How it is assessed

One 1-hour exam after each of the 36 monthly modules (theory plus practical where appropriate). Exam time is included in the 624 hours.

Questions

About this program.

Who can join this program?

Class 10 pass; basic school-level mathematics and computer familiarity. The program is designed for students entering after Class 10.

How many hours is the program, and how is it structured?

It is 624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Each monthly module ends with a one-hour exam, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

Does the program guarantee a job or internship?

No. The curriculum aims at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but it does not guarantee an internship, employment or salary.

Which external certifications does the roadmap include?

The roadmap lists six external certifications: Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900 / AI-901); AWS Certified AI Practitioner; Google AI Essentials; NVIDIA DLI Fundamentals of Deep Learning; IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate; DeepLearning.AI Machine Learning Specialization. They are awarded by the certifying organisations, not by this program, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.

What laptop do students need?

The curriculum document gives minimum specifications: CPU: Intel i5 / Ryzen 5 or better; RAM: 16 GB minimum, 32 GB recommended; Storage: 512 GB SSD minimum, 1 TB recommended; GPU: Integrated GPU for basic ML; NVIDIA RTX 4060 8 GB+ recommended for the full roadmap; OS: Windows 11 + WSL2 or Linux.

What kinds of roles does this program prepare students for?

The curriculum lists entry-level roles such as Junior AI/ML Engineer, Junior Machine Learning Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, GenAI Engineer / Developer. Fit depends on the student's portfolio and on each employer's requirements.

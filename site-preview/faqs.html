LearningHow much time does it take each week?

The three-year programs run 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year (208 hours a year, 624 in total). The one-year AI Foundations course runs 2 guided hours a week (104 hours), plus 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.

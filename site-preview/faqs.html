CertificationDo the programs include certifications?

The programs prepare students for external certifications such as Microsoft AI-901, AWS AI Practitioner, CompTIA Security+ and others named on each program page. The certifying organisations award them, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.

See certification pathways
PrivacyHow is my child's personal data handled?

Students under 18 apply through a parent or guardian, who gives consent. We collect only what is needed to reply, use unticked consent boxes, and do not track or advertise to children. If you do not join, we delete enquiry details 12 months after our last contact. Student records are kept for the length of the program plus two years, so certificates can be verified, then deleted. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time, and we act on it within 30 days. Our grievance contact is Hussain, at Enquire@neuronetwrks.com, who replies within 7 working days.

Contact us about privacy
AdmissionsWho is eligible to join?

All five three-year programs are designed for students from Class 11 onwards. The six-month AI Foundation course is open from Class 9, with no prior coding, AI or cloud experience needed.

LearningHow much time does it take each week?

The three-year programs run 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year (208 hours a year, 624 in total). The six-month AI Foundations course runs 4 guided hours a week for 26 weeks (104 hours), plus 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

All questions →
Who can apply?

Students who have passed Class 10.

How many hours is the AI/ML program?

624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.

How are learners assessed?

Every month ends with a project and a one-hour exam. In AI Foundation the pass mark is 80%, with review and a retry below it.

Which external certifications does the AI/ML roadmap include?

Six: Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals, AWS Certified AI Practitioner, Google AI Essentials, NVIDIA DLI Fundamentals of Deep Learning, IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate and DeepLearning.AI Machine Learning Specialization. They are awarded by the certifying organisations, and passing is not guaranteed.

How long is each program?

AI Foundation is 104 hours over one year. The five specialisations are three years and 624 hours each.

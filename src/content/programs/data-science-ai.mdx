faqs:
  - q: Who can join this program?
    a: Class 10 pass; absolute beginners welcome. The program is designed for students entering after Class 10.
  - q: How many hours is the program, and how is it structured?
    a: 'It is 624 hours over three years (36 months): 4 hours a week for 52 weeks a year, or 208 hours a year. Each monthly module ends with a one-hour exam, and exam time is counted inside the 624 hours.'
  - q: Does the program guarantee a job or internship?
    a: No. The curriculum aims at job readiness through skills, hands-on practice, a GitHub record and a portfolio, but it does not guarantee an internship, employment or salary.
  - q: Which external certifications does the roadmap include?
    a: 'The roadmap lists six external certifications: Microsoft Certified: Azure Data Fundamentals (DP-900); HackerRank Python & SQL Certificates; Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate; IBM Data Science Professional Certificate; Microsoft AI-300 (Machine Learning Operations Engineer Associate), the current replacement for the retired DP-100; IBM Data Engineering Professional Certificate. They are awarded by the certifying organisations, not by this program, and passing an external exam is not guaranteed.'
  - q: What laptop do students need?
    a: 'Recommended laptop specifications (recommendations, not entry requirements): CPU: Intel Core i7 / Ryzen 7 or equivalent; RAM: 32 GB; Storage: 1 TB NVMe SSD; GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4060/5060, 8 GB VRAM+; Display: 14–16" Full HD/2K IPS.'
  - q: What kinds of roles does this program prepare students for?
    a: The curriculum lists entry-level roles such as Junior Data Analyst, Data Analyst, BI Analyst / Junior BI Developer, Junior Data Scientist. Fit depends on the student's portfolio and on each employer's requirements.

faqs:
  - q: Who is the AI Foundations course for?
    a: It is for learners who have passed Class 10 or equivalent, with no prior coding, AI or cloud experience. Learners should be able to read simple English instructions, use a browser and manage files.
  - q: How long is the course?
    a: It is 104 guided hours over 52 weeks, at 2 hours a week. Months 1 to 8 are core learning (64 hours), Months 9 and 10 prepare for Microsoft AI-901 (20 hours), and Months 11 and 12 prepare for AWS AIF-C01 (20 hours).
  - q: Will I be certified, and does the course guarantee a pass?
    a: The course prepares learners for two external exams, Microsoft AI-901 and AWS AIF-C01, but does not guarantee passing. Certification is awarded by Microsoft or AWS only after the learner passes their exam.
  - q: Does this make me an AI/ML engineer?
    a: No. It is a beginner applied-AI foundation, not a full software engineering course, and it does not qualify a learner as an AI/ML engineer.
  - q: What do I need to take part?
    a: A laptop or desktop with internet and a browser, able to install Python or use a browser-based notebook. A phone alone is not enough for the programming labs. Plan 10 to 15 minutes of homework after each lecture.
